How It Works

  1. Before every round, the server generates a random secret seed and publishes its SHA-256(secretSeed + roundId) as the public hash — this is shown in the round info bar.
  2. The crash point is derived from HMAC-SHA256(secretSeed, roundId). The first 8 hex bytes are converted to a decimal, then the formula 0.99 / (1 − u) is applied (where u = decimal / 2^32). If the result is divisible by 101, the house wins at 1.00×.
  3. After the round crashes, the secret seed is revealed. You can paste it here along with the public hash to confirm the crash point was predetermined and not changed after bets were placed.
